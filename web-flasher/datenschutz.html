4. USB communication and settings

After you grant access to a port, your browser communicates locally with the ESP32 using Web Serial. The flash function transfers firmware to the connected device. It does not upload device logs, MAC addresses, Wi-Fi passwords or Jenkins credentials to a server. Your browser manages port permissions; you can revoke them there.

The website does not read saved settings. When the erase option is unchecked, the settings and branding regions are excluded from writing. “Erase all device data” clears the flash before installation. “Erase device” clears the entire flash without installation. Both require your confirmation. Downloads and connection data are processed as described in Sections 2 and 3.

5. Cookies, analytics and local storage

This website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising services, external fonts or browser storage to track visits. The flash library is only loaded when connecting. The operator does not create profiles. Your browser manages its technical cache and Web Serial permissions.

Messages are deleted once the enquiry has been resolved and no retention obligation or other necessity remains.

External links are opened only when you click them. The destination provider’s privacy notices apply on that website; it receives technical connection data when you visit.
